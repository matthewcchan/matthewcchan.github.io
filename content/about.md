I am currently a [Mahan Postdoctoral Fellow](https://www.fredhutch.org/en/research/divisions/public-health-sciences-division/research/computational-biology/mahan-fellowship.html) with [Arvind Rasi Subramaniam](https://www.fredhutch.org/en/faculty-lab-directory/subramaniam-arvind.html) and [Melody G. Campbell](https://www.fredhutch.org/en/faculty-lab-directory/campbell-melody.html) in the [Basic Sciences Division](https://www.fredhutch.org/en/research/divisions/basic-sciences-division.html) at the [Fred Hutch Cancer Center](https://www.fredhutch.org/en.html). My current research involves using structural, genomic, and computational approaches to study mRNA translational regulation and membrane receptor signaling.

I completed my Ph.D. in [Chemical Engineering](https://www.chbe.illinois.edu) at the [University of Illinois, Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/) in [Diwakar Shukla's lab](https://chbe.illinois.edu/directory/profile/diwakar). My thesis research focused on using molecular dynamics simulations and Markov state models to study the conformational dynamics and regulatory mechanisms of membrane transport protiens.

Prior to my graduate studies, I received my Bachelor of Science in [Biomedical Engineering](https://www.uab.edu/engineering/bme/) and [Applied Mathematics](https://www.uab.edu/cas/mathematics/) at [The University of Alabama at Birmingham](https://uab.edu/). I conducted my undergradute research with Dr. Margaret A. Johnson in the [Department of Chemistry](https://www.uab.edu/cas/chemistry/) investigating functional mechanisms of poly(ADP-ribose) polymerases and coronaviral non-structural proteins using solution NMR.
